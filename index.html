Find out more about the 2026 Senedd election
When is the 2026 Welsh election?

The Senedd election will be held on 7 May 2026.

How many constituencies are there now?

Wales now has 16 constituencies (down from 40). Each constituency elects 6 Members, making 96 Members total.

How many Members will the Senedd have?

The Senedd will have 96 Members from the 2026 election (up from 60).

How has the voting system changed?

You now get one vote to choose a political party or independent candidate, rather than two separate ballots. Seats reflect the percentage of votes each party receives (closed proportional list system).

Can candidates from anywhere stand in 2026?

No, anyone standing for election must live in Wales.

Are Senedd constituencies the same as Westminster constituencies?

No, Senedd constituencies are different. The new Senedd constituencies were created by pairing the 32 UK Parliament constituencies for Wales.

How often will elections happen now?

From 2026, Senedd elections will happen every four years (instead of every five years).
